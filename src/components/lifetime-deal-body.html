Frequently asked questions

Before you lock it in.

What counts as a stored session?+

A session is one saved Disbug capture. The allowance is shared by the workspace, including reports submitted through the widget. Permanently deleting an older session frees a slot for a new capture.

Is this really a one-time payment?+

Yes. The displayed price is paid once for lifetime access to the users, session allowance, and features included in the selected package.

Can I upgrade from 1 user to 5 or 10 later?+

You can stack a higher package while the founding lifetime offer remains available. After the offer closes, lifetime upgrades may no longer be sold.

Do anonymous reporters consume paid user seats?+

No. Anonymous reporters are included with every plan and never consume dashboard seats. Each submitted report counts toward the workspace's stored-session allowance.

What does fair-use AI include?+

The Business tier includes AI-generated titles and voice-to-text descriptions within a monthly fair-use allowance. This keeps ongoing AI costs sustainable without changing your one-time license.

How does the 60-day guarantee work?+

Use Disbug with your real workflow for up to 60 days. If it does not help your team capture and resolve bugs faster, contact us for a full refund.
