No business is immune to the negative impacts of customer feedback. When customers have something negative to say, it can have a damaging impact on your brand and business. That's why it's important to have customer feedback tools at your disposal, so you can deal with feedback quickly and effectively. Here are a few customer feedback tools that are commonly used by businesses of all sizes.

### [Disbug](/)

Disbug is a visual feedback tool that improves the workflow in software development teams and also tightens the user/ client feedback process. The Disbug widget allows users/ clients to report an issue directly from the browser in just a click. While the user reports a bug in such ease, Disbug captures all the technical logs in the background and attaches it with the report while automatically submitting it to their project management tool.

Disbug also enhances the workflow in QA teams with it's chrome extension. A tester can report a bug with audio and video annotations, screenshots, screen recording and technical logs in just a click.

Disbug would be a perfect tool for software developers, QA testers, designers and customer success. It is the best way to tighten your feedback tool for free.

####

Features:

- **Disbug widget**The widget automatically captures a screenshot, which the user can annotate before submitting the report. A session replay of 1 min is attached to the report to provide additional clarity for the developer.

- **Disbug chrome extension**The chrome extension allows testers/ reporters to create a detailed bug report a bug with all the technical information in just a click. This cuts off all the back and forth conversation among teams regarding bugs.

- **UI/UX review with Disbug**Disbug has a new feature to provide pitch perfect design feedback. With this feature, one can edit/ comment on any element on the live webpage.

### SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is a unique platform that allows businesses to collect customer feedback in a variety of ways. From online surveys to interactive questionnaires, SurveyMonkey has something that will suit your needs. Here are some of the most popular features of SurveyMonkey that can help you gather customer feedback:

1\. Online Surveys - With online surveys, businesses can collect feedback from a large number of customers in one fell swoop. This is perfect for getting quick and accurate feedback from a wide range of people.

2\. Interactive Questionnaires - Questionnaires are a great way to get more detailed feedback from your customers. With questions that are specific to your business, you can get a better understanding of what they like and don't like.

3\. Customer Feedback Reports - Once you have collected feedback through surveys and questionnaires, you can create customer feedback reports to share with your team and future customers. This will help you identify areas where you need to improve your business practices.

4\. Customer Engagement Tools - With SurveyMonkey's powerful engagement tools, you can keep your customers engaged and interested in your business by sending them periodic surveys, email campaigns, or special offers.

### Hubspot

Hubspot is a leading customer feedback platform that helps businesses collect and analyze feedback from their customers. Hubspot offers a variety of features to help businesses get the most out of their feedback data, including:

1\. A customer feedback scoring system that helps businesses identify the most important feedback and prioritize it for action.

2\. A message queue that allows businesses to send messages to customers in order to collect feedback (and also apologize for any inconvenience caused).

3\. A blog that provides tips and advice on how to use Hubspot's customer feedback tools.

4\. A forum where businesses can ask questions, share tips, and connect with other business owners.

All of these features make Hubspot a valuable resource for businesses of all sizes - from small startups to multinational corporations. Plus, its user-friendly interface makes it easy for even inexperienced business owners to get started using its tools.

### TrustPilot

TrustPilot is a great feedback tool with features that can help you to improve the quality of your online reviews. Here are some of the features that are available on TrustPilot:

1\. Review Ratings - This feature allows you to see how your reviews are rated by other users. You can also use this data to optimize your reviews so that they are more likely to be rated positively.

2\. Review Comments - This feature allows you to reply to reviews and provide additional information or clarification. This can be helpful if a review is inaccurate or incomplete.

3\. Feedback Reports - This feature gives you insights into the quality of your reviews, comment replies, and more. It helps you identify areas where you need to work on improving your review management process.

4\. Feedback Score - This feature allows you to see how well your overall review management process is performing based on various metrics such as feedback volume, feedback accuracy, and review engagement.

All of these features are useful for improving the quality of your online reviews and making sure that they are reliable and trustworthy.

### Google forms

Google forms can be a great way to get feedback from your customers or employees. The form can be embedded on your website or app, and users can fill it out and submit it directly from the form.

Here are a few of the features that Google Forms has to offer:

1\. Forms can be filled out anonymously - This is great for collecting feedback from customers who may not feel comfortable giving their name.

2\. Forms can be completed automatically - This is perfect for collecting feedback on products or services.

3\. Forms can be sent as email notifications - This is a great way to keep your customers informed about important changes or updates to your product or service.

4\. Forms can be tracked and analysed - This helps you find out which questions are being asked most, and which areas need more attention.

5\. Forms can be sent as surveys - This is a great way to gather feedback on topics such as customer satisfaction, product features, or anything else that you deem important.

Forms are a great way to get feedback from your customers or employees, and they have many features that make them an indispensable tool for gaining insight into how your customers think and feel about your product or service.
