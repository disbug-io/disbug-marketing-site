### Criteria for selecting product management tools

There are a number of things to consider when selecting a product management tool, including the best features for your business, the features that are most important to you, and the price range that is comfortable for you. Here are some of the key factors to keep in mind:

1\. Features - Make sure to evaluate the features of each product management tool before making a decision. Are there features that are particularly important to you? Do the tools offer enough functionality?

2\. Price range - As with any purchase, it is important to find a product management tool that is within your budget and comfortable for you. The price range should also be taken into account when selecting a tool - some products may be more expensive than others, but offer more features.

3\. Ease of use - It is important to find a product management tool that is easy to use and navigate. This will help you spend less time learning the ropes and more time running your business effectively.

### GitHub

If you're looking for a product management tool that's easy to use and widely available, GitHub is an excellent choice. It allows you to manage your project files and repositories, as well as track changes made to them. This helps you keep track of all the changes that are made to your product development process, and makes it easier for other team members to collaborate on the project.

Some features of GitHub that are helpful when managing a product development project include:

1\. Its pull request feature allows you to accept or reject changes made to your project files. This helps to ensure that all changes are properly reviewed and validated before they're committed to the repository.

2\. Its merge feature allows you to combine multiple changes made to different parts of your project into one single file. This helps prevent any misunderstandings or conflicts between team members, and ensures that the final product is consistent and error-free.

3\. Its issue tracking capabilities allow you to track bugs and other issues that may arise during the development process. This helps you to identify and fix any problems as they occur, so that your product is completed on time and meets all of its specifications.
