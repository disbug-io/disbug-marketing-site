This blog lists the 10 best bug tracking tools that you can use in 2021. It offers a description of its features, integrations, pricing, and its pros & cons. Find all the insights you need to choose a tool that fulfills all your requirements.

But before we get to the bug tracking tools, let’s discuss why we really need them.

Currently, people and businesses are becoming increasingly dependent on software solutions for almost everything. But a bug can greatly hinder their tasks. While it could be a small annoyance for some, it can also lead to losses for many businesses and individuals.

The best way to avoid it is by thorough testing in the early development process. The teams will have enough time to identify and fix all bugs. But to do that, they need comprehensive but easy-to-read bug reports.

You will encounter bugs in the development process, but they don’t have to be difficult to deal with. Fortunately, there are plenty of bug tracking tools that can help you streamline the process and bring transparency to the entire process.

### **Best Bug Tracking Tools**

Now that you know the importance of bug tracking, it’s time we look at the best options out there. Let’s dive in.

#### Disbug

![Bug report with screen recording and technical logs- Disbug](/static/blog/images/the-top-10-bug-tracking-tools-for-2021-public-fff1833a6dfc.png)

**Bug report with screen recording and technical logs- Disbug**

[Disbug](/) is one of the best bug tracking tools out there. It can help you create detailed bug reports that contain all information related to the bug. The intuitive interface lets you add console logs, network logs, local storage, user clicks, and technical logs with ease. Just install the Disbug chrome extension and record visual proof with a screencast.

It can significantly boost communication with your development team and enhance team collaboration. It is fairly simple to use and even non-developers can take advantage of it.

- Create detailed bug reports with ease.

- Chrome extension for capturing bugs with screen recording

- Capture technical information such as browser, OS, and display resolution.

- Integrations for Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Asana, and more for ease of reporting bugs.

#### eTraxis

![eTraxis dashboard](/static/blog/images/the-top-10-bug-tracking-tools-for-2021-public-c7a619daa240.png)

**eTraxis dashboard**

[eTraxis](https://www.etraxis.com/) is an open-source bug tracking system that is multilingual. By centralizing all project activity, eTraxis provides a customizable framework for managing projects involving various organizations. Users and projects can be created and viewed at the same time as problems are assigned. The ability to create your own workflows is an added benefit of this system.

Bug tracking is a critical tool for any business, and the benefits of using a bug tracking software are numerous.
