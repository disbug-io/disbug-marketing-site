### What is Usersnap?

The Usersnap feedback and bug tracking tool helps web and mobile teams collect, manage and act on customer feedback. It is a solution to collaborate with your customers directly in the browser and automatically capture, organize and share voice of customer insights with your team.

### Usersnap Pricing

**Basic $9/mo 2 members 1 feedback project** This plan includes: - Unlimited reporters - Micro surveys - Screenshots - Customer care replies - Add a team member - Add a project

**Startup $69/mo 10 members 5 feedback projects** This plan includes: Everything in Basic, plus: - Integrate with 4000+ apps

**Company $129/mo 15 members 15 feedback projects** This plan includes: Everything in Startup, plus: - Browser error recording - Custom widget design - Remove branding
