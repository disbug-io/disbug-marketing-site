Bug tracking isn't just a chore - it's your chance to get your work done right, on time and under budget. Disbug is a bug tracking software that helps you to manage your software development and bug reporting tasks. It provides you with an easy to use interface and powerful functionalities that let you quickly report and fix the bugs that matter to you. Let's look at 5 of the best features that you need to know about this tool.

### 5 Best Features of Disbug

#### Screen capturing and screen recording:

Reporting bugs is a time-consuming process. It's important to report your bug quickly, but it can be difficult when you don't know how to do so properly. Developers spend hours and days working on their code, only for the end user to report that they have found a bug in the application or website they are using.

With Disbug, you can take screenshots of your desktop or web browser without leaving any trace behind! This makes reporting bugs quick and easy for both developers and users alike - saving everyone valuable time while also ensuring that everything is being done correctly from start to finish!

#### Captures technical logs:

No one likes bugs. You spend hours fixing them, only to find out that they've come back. Bugs are annoying and stressful. There's no way around it - you have to deal with them!

With Disbug, a developer can track down and reproduce the bugs in your website or application using logs of all the actions taken by users and browsers while reporting bugs on your site as well as their browser information such as console logs, network logs, screen size, operating system etc. This information can help you to debug the bug and find out where in your code it originated. It's also helpful when trying to reproduce a bug - you'll know exactly what steps to take in order for it to happen again.

#### Edit live websites:

It's a pain to edit content on the web. The editing process is tedious, time-consuming, and can be error prone. If you want to add more value for your customers by providing them with an interactive experience that enables them to interact with your brand, then Disbug is what you need!

Disbug is a live editing tool that allows you to do more than just share and discuss! With this powerful feature, you can add comments and annotate in real-time while you're viewing them within your browser. This means that not only will everyone see what's being discussed - but they'll be able to contribute as well! It's easy for both developers and marketers alike - they can make changes in real-time without leaving their browser window or taking screenshots of their work.

#### Your own widget:

Bugs are a fact of life. They can be frustrating and annoying, but they're also the reason that your website works as it should. Testing is an essential part of building a great product, but it's often neglected in favor of other tasks like design or development.

Disbugs makes bug reporting easy for everyone! It's quick to install and integrate with any web application so you can use it right away without writing any code at all! Just place our widget on your site and start collecting bugs - no need to switch tabs or open up browser extensions anymore! You can track all of the important information about every single one of your bugs in just a few clicks.

#### Powerful integrations:

Getting feedback from your team is hard. You have to wait for them to respond, then you send out a request again and hope that they actually get back to you.

Internal communication has never been easier! Disbug tightens your feedback loop so they can easily give and receive feedback on their projects. It also provides powerful integrations with project management tools like Jira, Trello, Slack, GitHub and GitLab so the whole process is seamless.
