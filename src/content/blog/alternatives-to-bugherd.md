Looking for an effective tool to manage your web application bugs? Check out these top 5 alternatives to BugHerd! Each of these solutions has its own advantages and disadvantages, but they all have the ability to help you track down and fix pesky bugs in your web applications.

### [Disbug](/)

[Disbug](/) is an online bug reporting platform that helps users to quickly and easily report issues and track the resolutions. It offers a user-friendly interface, and its extensive reporting capabilities make it a valuable tool for software developers, product managers, QA testers, and other professionals who work with software products.

There are a few different ways that you can go about Disbug - some people use it as an alternative to Bugherd, while others find it to be a more comprehensive tool. Here are some of the key features that make Disbug stand out from the competition:

1\. Disbug is easy to use - Whether you're tech savvy or not, Disbug will be easy for you to use. It has a user-friendly interface and can be accessed by all members of a software team from developer to client.

2\. Disbug offers comprehensive reporting - You'll be able to see detailed information about all of your Bugs, including the technical logs such as console logs, network logs, browser information etc.

3\. Unlike Bugherd, Disbug offers solution to various parts of the software team with it's chrome extension and a customized widget.

4\. With Disbug, you can edit and comment on live websites which makes the design feedback system more efficient.

Disbug is the better platform for bug reporting. It is more user-friendly than Bugherd, offers a more comprehensive reporting platform, and helps resolve reported bugs more quickly than Bugherd.

Here are a few of the most important features:

1\. Wrike has an easy-to-use interface that makes it easy to add, view, and track bugs.

2\. Wrike offers automatic email notifications that let you know when a bug is fixed or when it needs your attention.

3\. Wrike also offers an easy way to assign tasks to team members, so you can get your bugs fixed as quickly as possible.

4\. Wrike allows you to filter your bugs by category or severity, so you can find the ones that need the most attention right away.
