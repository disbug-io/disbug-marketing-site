When you start using Google Chrome, you soon realise that it makes your life so much easier! It comes with a slew of advanced tools to facilitate productivity and save you time. But, there's one that you've probably not heard of – extensions.

Yes, extensions are added to Chrome to boost your experience further; they provide new features, extra tools, and let you do just about everything faster.

[Disbug](/)

---

[Disbug](/signup/) is a must-have chrome extension for software teams. It is a visual feedback tool that cuts off the boring bug reporting process and tightens the internal QA, customer and client feedback loop.

With it's various features including annotated screenshots, screen recordings, automatic updation of technical logs, live website edit features and the handy widget, Disbug is one of the most user-friendly and efficent visual feedback tools.

Disbug is proven to save upto 4 hours a day for each software team leading to immese productivity and work efficiency!

#### Disbug Pros

- It is simple, easy to use, and quick to set up.

- Has deep integrations with project management tools where each bug is uploaded as a ticket.

- The screencast and screenshot features eliminate the long and tiring bug reports.

- Improves the overall productivity of the team by saving time, energy and money spent.

- Reduces the back and forth calls between QA and developer

#### Disbug Cons

- Software development and QA teams benefit the most with Disbug.

- The tool is relatively new in the market.

### Awesome Screenshot

Awesome Screenshot is for creating different types of screenshots and screen records- full page, selected portion or window.

It has got intuitive user interface with lots of features to edit screenshot/ screen record before saving. You can add texts, arrows, circles, rectangles etc. on the captured screenshot and easily share it on social media. They also allow you to integrate the tool with various project management platforms like Trello, Jira, Asana etc.

#### Awesome Screenshot & Screen Recorder Pros:

- Flexible editing features including blur and numerous annotation tools.

- 4K video record feature is impressive

- Easy conversion of videos into mp4 format

#### Awesome Screenshot & Screen Recorder Cons:

- Customer service is not upto the mark

- Video feature does not have enough annotation options compared to the screenshot feature

### Forest chrome extension

If you are looking for a way to monitor and control your energy consumption, then you should check out the Forest Chrome extension. This extension allows users to track their energy usage, set daily or monthly goals, and receive alerts when they reach threshold levels. It also has features that allow users to compare their energy consumption against other households in their neighborhood.

#### Forest pros:

- Easy to use: The Forest Chrome extension is easy to use and allows users to track their energy usage, set daily or monthly goals, and receive alerts when they reach threshold levels.

- Comprehensive data: Forest provides comprehensive data that allows users to compare their energy consumption against other households in their neighborhood.

- Highly customizable: The Forest Chrome extension is highly customizable and allows users to adjust the look and feel of the extension according to your own preferences.

#### Forest cons:

- Limited functionality: While Forest has a lot of features, it lacks some of the more advanced features found in other energy trackers.

- Costly: The Forest Chrome extension is pricey compared to other energy trackers on the market.
