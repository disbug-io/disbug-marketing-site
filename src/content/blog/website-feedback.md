### What are website feedback tools?

Website feedback tools are a great way to get user feedback on your website. They allow you to collect feedback from customers who have interacted with your website in some way, such as registering for an account, making a purchase, or leaving a review.

### The importance of website feedback: how feedback tools help grow your business

User feedback is essential for the growth of any business. By using website feedback tools, you can collect valuable feedback from your customers and use it to improve the quality of your website.

For example, if you have a product page on your website, collecting user feedback through surveys will help you to understand what features are most important to your audience. This information can then be used to create more effective product pages and marketing materials.

In addition, user feedback can help you to improve the overall quality of your website. ### Top 3 Website feedback tools

There are a number of different website feedback tools available, so it can be hard to decide which one is the best for you. However, we’ve compiled a list of some of the most popular options below.

1\. [Disbug](/signup/)

Disbug is a go-to website feedback tool developed specially for software teams. With Disbug, one can report any kind of bug or issue directly from the webpage without changing tabs. It improves feedback loop drastically and also tightens the internal QA.

2\. UserVoice

UserVoice is another free online tool that allows you to collect user feedback through surveys and comments. You can also manage all the comments and responses to surveys in one place, which makes it easy to track progress and make changes as needed.

3\. CoSchedule

CoSchedule is a paid online tool that allows you to create polls, gather data, and capture customer feedback in a variety of ways. You can also use CoSchedule for A/B testing, which is a technique used to test different versions of webpages or ads with the goal of finding the best version for your audience.
