Every organization needs to have a way of communicating and tracking projects. Project management tools help project managers keep their teams organized, on time, and on budget while supporting the entire project lifecycle. ### What Is Project Management Software?

Project management software is a computer program that helps managers plan, track, and manage projects. It includes features such as project planning, time tracking, resource allocation, and communication tools.

There are many different types of project management software available on the market today.

### Best Project Management Tools & Software in 2022

There are many different types of project management software available on the market, but some of the most popular include:

**Project management with GanttPro**
