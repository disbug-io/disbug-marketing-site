If you're anything like us, you're always looking for ways to optimize your team's productivity. And Trello is the perfect tool for that! In this post, we'll show you five Trello power-ups that will help your team work more efficiently and effectively. So bookmark this page and start using these tips today!

### [Disbug](/)

If you are part of software development team, Disbug is a bug reporting and a screen recording tool specifically designed for you. You can take a screenshot, record your screen, give a video walk-through of bugs, explain and narrate new features to developers, annotate while recording with various set of annotation tools and even integrate with your favorite project management tool like Jira, Trello, GitHub, Slack etc.

In addition to being able to explain and annotate the bugs with a video walk-through, it automatically captures all the technical logs, console logs, browser information, mouse and keyboard inputs clearly.

This improves the workflow, reduces stress and makes the team extremely productive and increases team collaboration. Disbug is a must-have tool as it makes your life simpler, bug reporting easier and work more fun.

**Disbug Pros:**

- It is simple, easy-to-use and quick to setup.

- Has deep integrations with project management tools where each bug is uploaded as a ticket.

- The screencast and screenshot features eliminate the long and tiring bug reports.

- Improves the overall productivity of the team by saving time, energy and money spent.

- Reduces the back and forth calls between QA and developer

**Disbug Cons:**

- Software development and QA teams benefit the most with Disbug.

- The tool is relatively new in the market.

### Testim

Testim is a platform that makes it easy for developers to build and run tests for their project. Whether you are working on a client website or an internal application, you can use the Testim platform to create, manage, and execute automated functional tests – all with the help of artificial intelligence – allowing you to save time and effort.

#### Testim Pros:

- It is a comprehensive platform that covers all the testing needs for web and mobile applications.

- It has deep integrations with many popular project management tools, such as Trello and Asana.

- The Testim team can help you get started with testing quickly – the tool is easy to use even for non-technical users.

#### Testim Cons:

- The cost of Testim may be prohibitively expensive for some projects.

- It is not customizable.

### BigPicture

BigPicture is a project management solution that allows you to manage your team's most crucial components at the same time. Keys features like roadmap, scope (work breakdown structure), risks and resources, add flexibility to your development process and help you plan using either agile or waterfall methods.

#### BigPicture Pros:

- It allows you to manage your project with a single platform.

- It integrates with many popular tools, including Gantt Chart, Asana and Slack.

- The team can use the BigPicture tool for collaborative planning and tracking of projects.

#### BigPicture Cons:

- It is not free to use.

- It does not have a testing feature.

### TeamGantt

Working with a project management tool is key to knowing exactly where you stand and what needs to get done. With Teamgantt chart maker, you can organize your tasks on a timeline so everyone working on the project can see exactly when certain tasks should get done, who's working on them, and which aspect they are in charge of.

This helps to keep everyone in the loop of where their assignment falls as well as giving insight as to where there might be a possible problem or delay in the process. Since this online task tracking tool is equipped with user friendly functions it will only take you minutes to ensure everything goes smoothly for your next team collaboration!

#### TeamGantt Pros:

- Easy to create and update a project schedule.

- Flexibility of Teamgantt is excellent

- Visual nature of this interface makes it user-friendly

#### TeamGantt Cons:

- You can't keep a track of time with TeamGantt.

- Limited capability to zoom out on the timeline.

### BrowserStack

We all know that there is no way to test a webpage in every device and browser combo possible. Nor do we really have time to go through this tedious exercise. That is why BrowserStack has been created; as a cross-browser compatibility testing service.

The services provided by BrowserStack allows anyone to check if their site looks fine and functions perfectly across thousands of real mobile and desktop devices in the cloud without having to install any software on their computer or device, making this platform an ideal solution in the age of responsive design and DevOps.

#### BrowserStack Pros:

- You can run multiple tests with parallelization

- You can check the website in multiple browsers in one place

- You can quickly and easily switch between devices and operating systems

#### BrowserStack Cons:

- Setting up Browserstack could be confusing

- The price is quite high

### Closing thoughts

In the age of collaboration and project management, it is essential to have a tool that can efficiently track tasks, deadlines and interactions between team members. Each platform has its own unique pros and cons which ultimately depends on your specific needs as a business or individual. Ultimately, the most important factor is to choose the right tool for your team.
