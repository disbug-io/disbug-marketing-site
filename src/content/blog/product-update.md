Here at Disbugs, we love bugs! No, not the pesky kind that eat your crops and crawl into your ears. The ones you find in your website are valuable because they’re what make it work - but let’s face it, they can also be pretty frustrating when they keep popping up. Testers do their best to squash those little critters out of existence by making sure the functionality works as it should. They help the developers so that bugs don’t get carried over from project to project and web app to web app.

The Disbug team is excited to announce two new features in the upcoming release of our software: widget and live edit feature.

#### What is the Disbug widget?

Disbug widget is a great solution for quickly getting feedback on what people see in your website and we’re excited to announce it's available now.

With Disbug's widget feature, you can effortlessly report bugs directly from your web page. And with smart feedback on how we can improve user experience with bug reports - there's no reason not to try us out!

Disbug makes it easy for developers and website owners alike by providing an elegant solution that seamlessly integrates into their existing workflow and development process. The resulting product is much more than just a bug reporting tool; it creates a seamless interface between customers, designers, developers & management teams in order to increase productivity while reducing customer support costs as well as overall time spent on resolving issues!

#### What is the live edit feature?

It's hard to get real feedback on your design work. Whatever details you add to your report, you can never provide the clarity for the developer on design changes. That is why we are launching the new "Live edit" feature where you can edit the designs/UI on any live webpage. You can edit the content, color, font, space on web objects directly. You can annotate/ edit directly on webpage instead of sending screenshots/ videos.

Design with elegance, style and no coding: just click and adjust any page element to your preference in one fell swoop!
