As web development and design agencies grow in size and complexity, it becomes increasingly important for clients to be able to work together on projects. Unfortunately, traditional tools like email and chat don't always provide the best platform for collaboration. That's why we've put together this list of 9 client collaboration tools that can help your team work more efficiently and effectively. From project management software to task managers, make sure you're using the right tool for the job!

### 1\. Trello

Trello is a popular project management tool that can be used to manage tasks, boards, and lists of items. It makes it easy for team members to collaborate on projects by allowing them to assign tasks and track progress together. Plus, Trello is free to use so you won't have to worry about spending any money on software that could potentially be more important elsewhere in your agency's budget.

#### Benefits of using Trello

1\. Increased productivity - By using Trello, you can get more done in less time by sharing and organizing tasks into boards and lists. This makes it easier to find information and track progress.

2\. Improved communication - With Trello, you can easily communicate with your team members by adding comments to task cards and sharing boards with them. This helps to ensure that everyone is on the same page and aware of what's going on.

3\. Easier project management - Trello helps to keep track of all aspects of your projects, from planning to execution. This makes it easier to get everything done on time and under budget.

4\. Reduced stress - By working in a collaborative environment, you can avoid the stress of dealing with unanswered or incomplete tasks. With Trello, everything is always moving forward and everyone is aware of the latest developments.

5\. Increased efficiency - By using Trello, you can easily streamline your workflow and improve your efficiency overall. This not only reduces the amount of time you need to spend on tasks each day, but it also boosts your morale as you work towards goals together as a team!

### 2\. SmartTask

#### Benefits of using SmartTask

1\. Accurate tracking - With SmartTask, you will always know exactly what you have accomplished in each hour, day, or week. This will help you to stay on track and achieve your goals more efficiently.

2\. Customizable reports - You can customize your reports to get a detailed overview of your work performance. This will help you to identify any areas where you need to improve and make adjustments accordingly.

3\. Powerful collaboration tools - With SmartTask, you can easily share your reports and data with co-workers or clients for feedback and collaboration. This will help to improve communication and teamwork within your team.

4\. Automatic time-tracking - With SmartTask, time-tracking is automatically handled for you, so there is no need to keep track of time manually. This makes it easier for you to focus on the task at hand and get more work done in less time!
