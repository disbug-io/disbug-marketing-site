### [Disbug](/)

![Effective feedback with Disbug](/static/blog/images/alternatives-to-marker-io-public-c20493e9ecc5.png)

**Effective feedback with Disbug**

Disbug is an online bug reporting platform that helps users to quickly and easily report issues and track the resolutions. It offers a user-friendly interface, and its extensive reporting capabilities make it a valuable tool for software developers, product managers, QA testers, and other professionals who work with software products.

There are a few different ways that you can go about Disbug - some people use it as an alternative to [Marker.io](http://marker.io/), while others find it to be a more comprehensive tool. Here are some of the key features that make Disbug stand out from the competition:

1\. Disbug is easy to use - Whether you're tech savvy or not, Disbug will be easy for you to use. It has a user-friendly interface and can be accessed by all members of a software team from developer to client.

2\. Disbug offers comprehensive reporting - You'll be able to see detailed information about all of your Bugs, including the technical logs such as console logs, network logs, browser information etc.

3\. Unlike Bugherd, Disbug offers solution to various parts of the software team with it's chrome extension and a customized widget.

4\. With Disbug, you can edit and comment on live websites which makes the design feedback system more efficient.

Disbug is the better platform for bug reporting. It is more user-friendly than Bugherd, offers a more comprehensive reporting platform, and helps resolve reported bugs more quickly than Bugherd.

### Rollbar

![Rollbar dashboard](/static/blog/images/alternatives-to-marker-io-public-f94d9a9c44f0.png)

**Rollbar dashboard**

Rollbar is a bug reporting tool that offers a few features that make it a better alternative to [marker.io](http://marker.io/). First and foremost, Rollbar offers unlimited storage for all your reports, which is great for capturing even the most complex issues.

One of the great benefits of using Rollbar is that it offers users with a "global view" of all their issues. This means that users can see issues in their web apps and mobile apps from anywhere in the world. In addition, Rollbar offers users with instant feedback on the severity of an issue, as well as suggestions on how to fix it.

Some of the key features of Rollbar include:

1\. Real-time data collection - Collects data from your web applications in real time, so you can track and diagnose errors as they occur.

2\. Monitoring and analysis - Provides detailed insights into the performance and health of your web applications, so you can quickly correct issues and optimize your systems.

3\. Quick response times - Gets you results back quickly so you can make informed decisions about how to address problems.

4\. Integrated support for popular languages and frameworks - Supports a wide range of languages and frameworks, so you can quickly find the right solution for your needs.

5\. Automated reporting - Generates detailed reports that help you identify and fix problems with your web applications quickly and easily.

### TestRail

![Testrail dashboard](/static/blog/images/alternatives-to-marker-io-public-f571499765d7.jpg)

**Testrail dashboard**

TestRail is a bug reporting tool that can be used to report issues with your websites and applications. It has a wide range of features that make it an optimal choice for developers, testers, and QA professionals. Some of the features that make TestRail stand out include its easy-to-use interface, automated test execution, and comprehensive reporting capabilities.

With TestRail, you can:

1\. Create, run, and manage automated tests using the same tools you use to develop your applications.

2\. Run your tests on multiple types of software platforms and devices.

3\. Easily share test results with your team members for feedback and collaboration.

4\. Access test results from any device or location with an internet connection.

5\. Save time by automating common tasks such as running tests multiple times or capturing screen images during testing sessions.

6\. Integrate with other Continuous Integration (CI) tools to speed up the testing process even more.

7\. Analyze the results of your tests to identify and fix errors early in the development cycle.

8\. Easily create reports that highlight the most important information about your tests and application development progress overall.
