### What is Bugherd?

BugHerd is a visual website feedback tool that makes it easier to manage bugs, issues and feedback from your team and clients.

BugHerd facilitates collaboration with clients and team members on projects by facilitating the use of screenshots in order to conduct quick exchanges about issues that need to be addressed for your project.

### Bugherd Pricing

**Standard$39/mo5 members(additional member for $8/member)** This plan includes:- Unlimited Projects- Unlimited Guests- Screenshots and integrations- Public feedback widget- 10GB storage

**Premium$129/mo25 members(additional member for $8/member)** This plan includes: Everything in Standard, plus:- Video feedback- Custom branding/logo- Guest see other guest feedback- Member only commenting- 50GB storage

**Deluxe$229/mo50 members(additional member for $8/member)** This plan includes: Everything in Premium, plus:- 150GB storage
