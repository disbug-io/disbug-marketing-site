### Web annotation tool

Website annotation tools are a great way to help you annotate and annotate your website.

Annotating your website can be very helpful in several ways. For example, it can help you keep track of changes that have been made to your website, it can help you find and fix errors, and it can help you understand how people are using your website.

There are many different annotation tools available on the market, so it is important to choose one that is suited for your needs.

### Top 6 website annotation tools

#### [Disbug](/)

Disbug is a web annotation tool specially designed for software teams. With Disbug, you can produce annotated screenshots and screen recordings with technical logs automatically attached to your reports. This tool not only helps you produce annotated screenshots but also helps you improve your internal QA and tighten your customer feedback loop.

Their recent update comes with a widget that can be placed in your website and users can report any issues without leaving the tab. They also have a live- edit feature where you can edit anything on a live webpage. This helps web designers and UI developers.

#### Usersnap

Usersnap is a tool for capturing screenshots, annotating over them, and then sharing the images with a team. An individual can install this tool on their computer or browser via a browser extension or code snippet. It can also be used as a way of getting customer feedback on websites or even as part of the bug tracking process to make improvements.

#### PageProofer

PageProofer is a sticky note for your website that allows your team to annotate and leave feedback on it. Every member of your team can communicate together based on their tasks, which are assigned to them via priority, while also being able to handle issues on time. PageProofer is special because it works well with responsive websites and operating systems as well as other browsers. No other tool currently serves more than two companies at once.

#### Scrible

Scrible is a research resource that you can use and engage with from the Chrome Web Store. With this handy tool all you need to do is simply press the red icon marked by an ‘s’ in your browser, and then drag this out of the way before hitting your Scrible dashboard where you can verify annotations, share these with other users, organize these into archives and tag your saved items. These saved items are also easily searchable via more advanced filters systems on their own!

#### Bugherd

BugHerd is a visual website feedback tool that makes it easier to manage bugs, issues, and feedback from your team and clients. BugHerd lets clients and team members leave feedback notes directly on a website, just like a sticky note, that includes technical information like screenshots, bug descriptions, and other information that both developers or designers will need in order to solve the problem quickly.

#### **Diigo**

While exploring, Diigo allows you to collect online resources and annotate on web pages and PDFs. These notes may be shared, bookmarked, stored in a personal library, and even preserved. Diigo, like the majority of the other utilities, is a plug-in. There are both free and premium options available. The free plan, on the other hand, has advertisements and a restriction on bookmarks and webpage highlights. In the free version, there is also no possibility to save images or PDFs after annotating them. The Business plan includes collaboration features. There's also a dashboard for the admin console.
