So whether you're curious about trying out one of these options, or you already use one of them and want to share your thoughts, read on!

### [Disbug](/)

![2 minute bug report with Disbug](/static/blog/images/the-top-10-bug-tracking-tools-for-2021-public-fff1833a6dfc.png)

**2 minute bug report with Disbug**

Disbug is an online bug reporting platform that helps users to quickly and easily report issues and track the resolutions. It offers a user-friendly interface, and its extensive reporting capabilities make it a valuable tool for software developers, product managers, QA testers, and other professionals who work with software products.

There are a few different ways that you can go about Disbug - some people use it as an alternative to [Marker.io](http://marker.io/), while others find it to be a more comprehensive tool. Here are some of the key features that make Disbug stand out from the competition:

1\. Disbug is easy to use - Whether you're tech savvy or not, Disbug will be easy for you to use. It has a user-friendly interface and can be accessed by all members of a software team from developer to client.

2\. Disbug offers comprehensive reporting - You'll be able to see detailed information about all of your Bugs, including the technical logs such as console logs, network logs, browser information etc.

3\. Unlike Userback, Disbug offers solution to various parts of the software team with it's chrome extension and a customized widget.

4\. With Disbug, you can edit and comment on live websites which makes the design feedback system more efficient.

Disbug is the better platform for bug reporting. It is more user-friendly than Userback, offers a more comprehensive reporting platform, and helps resolve reported bugs more quickly than Userback.

### JIRA

![Jira dashboard](/static/blog/images/alternatives-to-userback-public-53b24aa06a86.png)

**Jira dashboard**

While Userback is a popular tool for managing customer service and support, it does have some limitations. Jira has many of the same features, and can be a great alternative for managing projects. Here are some of the key reasons why Jira may be a better choice for your project:

1\. Jira is highly customizable - You can make Jira exactly the way you want it to look and function, making it easy to manage and control your projects.

2\. Jira integrates with many different tools and applications - Including Confluence, Bitbucket, Trello, Slack, and GitHub. This means that you can easily access information from your projects any time you need it.

3\. Jira offers real-time updates - So you always have up-to-date information on your projects, no matter where you are in the process.

4\. Jira is easy to use - Whether you're a beginner or an experienced project manager, Jira is sure to be easy to use and manage.

If you're looking for a tool that can help you manage your projects more effectively, then Jira may be a good option for you.

### FogBugz

![FogBugz dashboard](/static/blog/images/alternatives-to-userback-public-60b6e030d152.png)

**FogBugz dashboard**

Userback is a great tool for managing user accounts and profiles, but FogBugz may be a better alternative for some businesses. Here are some key reasons why:

1\. FogBugz integrates with more CMSs, including Shopify, WordPress, and Drupal.

2\. FogBugz offers more features for managing user profiles, including the ability to add custom fields, import data from other sources, and create custom reports.

3\. FogBugz is more user-friendly than Userback, making it easier for users to find and use the features they need.

4\. FogBugz offers better security features than Userback, including the ability to password protect profiles and logs of activity for each user.

Overall, FogBugz is a powerful tool that can help businesses manage user accounts and profiles efficiently and securely.

**Freshdesk dashboard**

Freshdesk is an excellent alternative to Userback for bug reporting.
