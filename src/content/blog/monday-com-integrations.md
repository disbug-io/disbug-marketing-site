### [Disbug](/)

Disbug integrates with Monday.com so teams can send captured bug reports and their technical evidence to the relevant project dashboard.

Disbug captures browser bug evidence such as recordings, screenshots, console logs, network activity, and user events for developers and coding agents. The integration keeps that evidence connected to the team's existing Monday.com workflow.

### Slack

Slack is a popular workplace messaging app that has recently become very popular for businesses. It offers many features that are beneficial for businesses, such as team messaging, file sharing, and meeting scheduling. Here are some more details about how slack works:

1\. Slack uses channels to organize messages. Channels are groups of messages that are automatically archived and searchable. This makes it easy to find the message you're looking for, no matter how deep into the conversation it is buried.

2\. Slack also offers direct messages (DMs). DMs are private conversations between two people that are not archived or searchable. This makes them perfect for sending confidential information between team members.

3\. Slack offers file sharing capabilities through its integrated file transfer client (FTX). FTX lets you securely share files with other members of your team without having to worry about security issues or data loss.

4\. Slack also offers meeting scheduling capabilities through its flexible agenda system. This system lets you easily create and manage meetings with your team, without having to worry about coordinating dates and times manually.

All of these features make slack a powerful tool for businesses of all sizes. If you're looking for a messaging platform that can help you streamline your work life.

Slack integrates with [Monday.com](http://monday.com/) to make it easy for employees to get the latest news and updates from the company in a single place. Just type /monday into Slack to see all the latest content from [Monday.com](http://monday.com/) right on your screen. You can also reply to any of the posts with your thoughts or feedback, or even start a discussion with your team.

### Google Calender

Google Calendar is a great tool to help you keep track of your appointments, events and tasks. It's easy to use and can be accessed from any device. Here are some quick tips on how to maximize the benefits of Google Calendar:

1\. Add your work, school and other activities - Including important dates and information about your work, school or other activities will make it easier for you to keep track of what's going on. This will also help you avoid conflicts and save time.

2\. Set a daily or weekly reminder - If you have a busy schedule, setting a reminder can be a lifesaver. You can easily set a reminder for yourself or for specific colleagues, so that everything is taken care of without any hassle.

3\. Create custom calendars - If you want more control over the types of events and tasks that are included in your calendar, you can create custom calendars with specific parameters set by you. This can be helpful if you have a lot of events that need to be coordinated between multiple people.

4\. Use filters - You can use filters to narrow down your calendar view to show only certain types of events, people or tasks. This can be helpful if you want to focus on specific areas of your life or work schedule.

Google Calendar is a great tool that can help make your life easier in many ways.

You can add Google Calendar events to [Monday.com](http://monday.com/)! To get started, go to [Monday.com/events](http://monday.com/events) and add your events. You can also specify when the event is open or closed, as well as the time zone it will be in. Then, you'll need to add the event to your Google Calendar account. Once it's added, you'll be able to view and manage the event from [Monday.com](http://monday.com/)!

### Todoist

Todoist is a popular to-dolist app that is used by millions of people all over the world. It has a wide range of features that make it an excellent choice for anyone looking for an efficient way to manage their tasks. Here are some of the most popular features of Todoist:

1\. Todoist integrates with Google Calendar and other third-party calendars to make it easy to add and organize tasks with dates and times.

2\. Todoist lets you create projects and sub-projects, which makes it easy to organize your tasks into manageable chunks.

3\. Todoist has a powerful search feature that lets you find any task or project quickly and easily.

4\. Todoist supports multiple languages and can be used in almost any country or language.

5\. Todoist is free to use and offers a wide range of premium features that are available for a fee. These include priority support, enhanced collaboration features, and more.

Todoist is an excellent choice for anyone looking for an efficient way to manage their tasks - it has tons of features that make it easy to get organized and stay on track.

[Monday.com](http://monday.com/) and Todoist are both compatible with each other. To use Todoist with [Monday.com](http://monday.com/), sign in to your [Monday.com](http://monday.com/) account, click on the gear icon in the top right corner of the screen, and select Settings. On the settings page, under "Integrations & Services," select Todoist. You'll then be able to add tasks from Todoist to your [Monday.com](http://monday.com/) list.
