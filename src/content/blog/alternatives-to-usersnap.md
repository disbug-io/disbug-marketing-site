2\. Disbug offers comprehensive reporting - You'll be able to see detailed information about all of your Bugs, including the technical logs such as console logs, network logs, browser information etc.

3\. Unlike Usersnap, Disbug offers solution to various parts of the software team with it's chrome extension and a customized widget.

4\. With Disbug, you can edit and comment on live websites which makes the design feedback system more efficient.

Disbug is the better platform for bug reporting. It is more user-friendly than Usersnap, offers a more comprehensive reporting platform, and helps resolve reported bugs more quickly than Usersnap.
