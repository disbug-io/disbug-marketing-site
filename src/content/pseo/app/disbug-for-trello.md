## Disbug for Trello

Terahog Labs

Disbug is a platform that helps you report bugs and crashes in an easy and convenient way. No more unclear and lengthy bug reports!

It offers a wide range of features that let users attach all the relevant information to their bug reports - whether they want to give a video-walkthrough, capture a screenshot along with network and console logs, include mouse and keyboard inputs, steps to reproduce the bug etc.

Disbug's Trello integration connects captured browser evidence to the relevant board, keeping the visual report and technical context with the team's project workflow.

#### Our Review:

Disbug is simple, easy to use, and quick to set up. Its Trello integration keeps screenshots, recordings, and technical evidence connected to the card developers will investigate.
