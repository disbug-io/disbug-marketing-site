## Snapz Pro

Ambrosia Software

0

Snapz Pro X is the ultimate Mac screen capture software for recording anything on your desktop and saving it to a QuickTime movie or screenshot.

Whether you want to record an application's demonstration, create a tutorial for others to follow, or just save an image of something funny that happened on your computer, Snapz Pro X is the tool to use. The tool lets you capture the whole screen or a smaller part, you can adjust the frame rate and has many other features like following the cursor and panning smoothly to new locations. In addition to this, Snapz provides a live preview feature where you can keep track of what is being recorded.

#### Snapz pro Pros:

- High-quality video capture
- The preview feature is useful
- Allows you to save in many formats including Quicktime

#### Snapz pro Cons:

- Doesn't compress screencast audio.
- The paid version is quite expensive.
