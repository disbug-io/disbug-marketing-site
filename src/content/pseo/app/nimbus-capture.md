## Nimbus Capture

Nimbus Web company

0

Nimbus Capture is your ultimate screen capture and video recording tool. It's simple, quick, and free to use on all of your devices.

Nimbus Capture allows you to record videos of your entire desktop or browser tabs, or create videos using your webcam. With Nimbus Capture, you can convert screenshots into high-quality videos or GIFs (like those that appear in Giphy), and export the result as a video file for uploading to YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, and more.

They also have a feature called Nimbus note which is great for organizing all your data in a single place. You can colour code, tag and sort all your data in an easy to find and customise to your needs.

#### Pros:

- Nimbus provides five different types of screenshots
- Keyboard shortcuts can be activated
- Simple and interactive user interface

#### Cons:

- Takes time saving the video to a particular storage
- There have been complaints of the crashing during recording leading to the loss of files
