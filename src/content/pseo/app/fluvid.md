## Fluvid

Fluvid

0

Fluvid is a platform for video communication. It lets you communicate with your team members, customers and other people over the internet through screen sharing & screen recording.

This way it's much easier to understand what they want or need without asking them questions. With Fluvids chrome extension you can, record, edit, add CTA to your video, upload on any platform etc.

In addition to this you can also protect your content with passwords and get video analytics on the performance of your videos.

#### Fluvid Pros:

- Very handy to use
- Good customer support
- Easy to collaborate and share among social networks
