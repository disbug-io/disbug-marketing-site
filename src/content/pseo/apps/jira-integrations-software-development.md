# Jira Integrations for Software Development

## Jira Integrations for Software Development

Jira is one among the best project management tools in the market today. From starting out as a bug tracker to being a crutial part of almost all of the companies these days, Jira has become so powerful. One of the reasons for its' success is due to the flexible integrations the software provides with other tools.

Especially with the whole remote work wave, it has been hard to keep track and be efficient at work for many of us. Jira provides integrations that comes handy for all such professionals, be it individuals or teams!

One such profession where Jira integrations can be best used of is Software Development!

Software developers have a lot on their hands from setting different tasks, checking the progress of work that's done by other team members, automating coding and what not.The last thing they need is for their tools to make their job harder. Fortunately, Jira Software has a host of integrations that can make their job easier.

This article looks at the **Top 6 Jira integrations for Software Development** that can be used to increase efficiency at your work.

Let's start exploring them!
